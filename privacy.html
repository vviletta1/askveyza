YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

Updated November 5, 2026

When you contact Ask.Veyza

You choose the business name, email address, website or profile, goals, and notes you share. They are used to respond to your inquiry and discuss the requested work. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive customer records.

How the contact options work

“Prepare my email” opens your own email app. You review and send the message yourself. “Copy inquiry” copies the message for you to send. Neither action submits the inquiry from this website.

When the form offers “Send my request,” the website submits your request through its server and Resend, the email-delivery provider, to violetta@askveyza.com. A success message means the provider accepted the email; it does not confirm inbox delivery. The form does not save a separate website database record. Submitted messages may remain in the email systems used to handle your request.

The owner workspace

The private Owner Studio encrypts business records in the browser where they are created. There is no cloud sync. Exported backups are downloaded files you control. The original access key is needed to decrypt them. The server uses a session cookie for owner authentication.

Website reviews request public pages and keep their findings in the owner’s browser records. Fictional examples are labeled and their practice changes are not saved.

Website delivery

Vercel hosts this website and processes requests to deliver pages and operate server functions. Fonts may load from Google Fonts. These services receive request information such as an IP address. Contact details and inquiry text are not put into website URLs by the direct-send form.

Questions or removal requests

Email violetta@askveyza.com to ask about information you have shared or request its removal. Identify the relevant message or business, and avoid sending additional sensitive information.
