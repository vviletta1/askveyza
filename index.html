Common questions

Simple answers before you decide.

What does my website plan include?

The pages and features listed in your chosen package. Managed subscriptions include hosting, SSL, and plan-based updates. Your proposal confirms revisions, support, timeline, and anything extra before payment.

Are marketing and tracking included in the monthly price?

Growth includes basic local SEO setup. Ongoing SEO work, social content, campaigns, connected reports, and custom tools are separately scoped options. Advertising, messaging usage, and paid software are additional when needed.

Can I see something before paying?

Yes. The first personalized website preview is free. You can also discuss a separately priced pilot for a specific improvement before choosing ongoing growth support.

What do you need from me?

Start with your business name, website or profile if you have one, and your main goal. Later we agree on services, prices, photos, and any account access. Keep passwords private; use the platform’s access invitations where available.

Are the trackers and Business Twin already connected?

No. The report is sample data, and the calculator runs only on the figures you enter. Live reporting, inbox syncing, scheduling, and automated messages require a separate supported connection and setup. We confirm availability and cost before including them in your proposal.

Can you guarantee customers or a top Google ranking?

No. We agree on work we can deliver and measure the records available. Search position and sales depend on many factors. Forecasts remain estimates, and reports distinguish inquiries, completed jobs, recorded sales, and unknown sources.

How soon can we get started?

We confirm a realistic delivery date after reviewing your pages, content, and any connections. Website work can be scoped separately from advanced integrations, so you can choose a manageable starting point.

What happens to my site if I leave?

Managed plans begin with a six-month term. After that, continue month to month or request a final handoff and arrange your own hosting. One-time purchases include the completed website package. Your proposal explains domain ownership, account access, exports, and any third-party licenses.

Will anything be posted or sent without my approval?

You approve public content, campaigns, and customer messages. Any later automation needs an agreed scope and supported account connection. Sending this inquiry does not authorize a campaign or start a subscription.

Do I have to scan or build my own website?

No. Violetta handles the review and website work. The public site lets you explore the service and prepare an inquiry. Research tools and prospect reviews are handled privately.
