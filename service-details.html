BEFORE WE START

Know what you are agreeing to.

A free preview request is an inquiry. It does not purchase a plan or enroll you in a subscription.

Your proposal comes first

Before paid work starts, your written proposal should identify the pages, features, content responsibilities, revision allowance, delivery timeline, and price. It should also state the number or allowance of ongoing updates, expected response times, and how larger requests are quoted.

Managed websites

The advertised subscription starts with a six-month term and a $199 setup fee. The minimum six-month totals are Starter $793, Growth $1,153, and Pro $1,693. Hosting and SSL are included during the managed subscription. Your proposal must explain billing dates, cancellation notice, and any charges before you accept.

Ownership and handoff

One-time website ownership starts at $999. Hosting, future updates, domains, and paid external services are separate. After a managed website’s initial six months, you may continue month to month or request the final handoff package. Confirm the included files, accounts, licenses, and transfer steps in your written agreement.

Additional services

Marketing work, assistant usage, account connections, advertising, and paid tools are scoped and quoted separately. Live analytics and account syncing are not connected in the example dashboard. Search rankings, inquiries, and sales are not guaranteed.

Questions? Ask Violetta before committing.
