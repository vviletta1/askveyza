THE REASON BEHIND THE REDESIGN

More than a new look.

Here’s what changed—and what each change is intended to make easier for a customer.

What we looked atStarting-point conceptProposed changeWhy it helps
First impressionGeneric welcome and small photoLarge visual, distinct brand, clear offerVisitors can understand the service quickly.
ServicesOne short list with “other services”Separate sections for recurring care and cleanupsCustomers can find the type of help they need.
Contact routeA small link after the main contentVisible links in navigation, hero, services, and closing sectionThe next step stays easy to find.
How it worksNo explanation of the processA simple three-step introductionCustomers know what to expect before asking.
Phone layoutBasic content without a designed mobile structureStacked cards, readable type, and clear touch targetsThe proposed design adapts to a smaller screen.

Design comparison only. No real website was audited, and no traffic, ranking, booking, or revenue improvement has been measured. For a business-specific report, the “before” view should use that business’s actual page, source URL, review date, and verified findings.
