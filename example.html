THE DESIGN DECISIONS

Three improvements a visitor can see.

  1. Services together: customers can compare the jobs offered without searching through posts.
  2. An obvious next step: the estimate section is linked from the top of the page.
  3. Space for real evidence: a production site would use authorized project photos and genuine customer feedback supplied by the owner.

No customer reviews, project photos, traffic figures, or sales results are fabricated for this example.
